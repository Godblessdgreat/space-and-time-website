01 — About the Studio

We are Space & Time.

Space & Time Design Concepts is a design and build architectural firm based in Lagos, Nigeria. We are an enthusiastic team of creative, intelligent, and inspired young professionals who believe in a balanced utilitarian approach to spaces — artfully conceptualized and crafted to a formidable standard of spatial arrangement.

Every space we touch carries our signature — precision, creativity, and a deep respect for how people experience the built environment. From residential developments to commercial office complexes, from Lagos to Kaduna, Port Harcourt, and Liberia, our portfolio demonstrates that great design transcends geography.

10 — Questions

Frequently asked.

Everything worth knowing before you reach out. If yours isn't here, send us a brief.

What types of projects do you take on?

Residential homes and developments, commercial offices, banking and corporate fit-outs, hospitality, and selective public-realm projects. If it shapes how people experience space, we're interested. Recent work spans Eco Bank, PETAN, private villas in Omole and Lekki, and residential development in Liberia.

Can I commission only renderings — or only construction?

Yes. Our three pillars work end-to-end together but are also offered standalone. Bring us a finished design to render, or a finished design to build — we'll meet you where you are.

How long does a typical project take?

Design alone runs 8–16 weeks. Visualization, 2–6 weeks. Construction varies with scope, site, and approvals. After our discovery call we send a phased programme with realistic dates — and stick to it.

Do you work outside Nigeria?

Yes. We've delivered residential work in Liberia and welcome briefs from across West Africa and beyond. Geography is logistics — design is design.

How do you price your work?

Architectural fees: a percentage of construction value, or a fixed fee for defined scopes. Visualization: per image or per package. Construction: by detailed bill of quantities. Always transparent, always documented.

How do we get started?

Send a brief through the form below, or email space8timed.c@gmail.com. We respond within two working days with a discovery-call invitation.
